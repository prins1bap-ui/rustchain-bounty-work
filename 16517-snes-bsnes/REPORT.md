## 3. Rerunnable harness

The complete rerunnable workflow is committed at:

`.github/workflows/bounty-16517-snes-bsnes.yml`

Measurement commit:

`6ebd776126e970501ddac5f9cf51a730bead47a5`

Successful workflow run:

`34867115249`

The workflow:

1. installs only the runtime and ROM-build dependencies;
2. downloads and SHA-verifies the pinned official bsnes binary;
3. checks out the exact SNES source SHA;
4. builds the canonical SlowROM/FastROM profile ROMs;
5. runs each ROM three independent times under bsnes;
6. waits for the cartridge `DONE` marker in bsnes SRAM;
7. runs upstream `tools/check_nn.py` against every SRAM result;
8. parses the ROM's profile block with upstream `tools/prof_nn.py` (`--fast` for FastROM);
9. checks repeated SRAM hashes for determinism; and
10. uploads all raw SRAM, logs, hashes and parsed reports as a workflow artifact.

This avoids converting CI elapsed time into emulated timing. The timing values below are generated by the canonical profiling ROM and decoded from SRAM by the upstream parser.
