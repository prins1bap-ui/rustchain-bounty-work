## Proposal operating rule

Do not spend Connects merely to increase activity. Submit only when the job remains open, the final posting still matches the public snapshot, the Connects cost is known, the deliverable can be completed primarily by ChatGPT/tools, and the proposal can be supported with a truthful proof asset.
