## Production notes

- **Visual hierarchy:** hook in the first line/frame, one idea per asset, CTA always last.
- **Mobile readability:** keep overlay copy under ~12 words per frame where possible; use large high-contrast type.
- **Footage:** original or properly licensed only. Avoid recognizable commercial meme stills so the pack can be published without dependence on third-party reaction images.
- **Accuracy:** describe RustChain as an experimental network and Proof of Antiquity as its design. Do not promise token value, profitability, or a specific multiplier without checking the current rules for the exact hardware.
- **Human CTA ladder:** curiosity (`learn`) → zero-commitment test (`dry run`) → project page. No account creation or purchase is required in the awareness-stage copy.
