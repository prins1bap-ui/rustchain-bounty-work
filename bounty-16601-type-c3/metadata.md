RustChain's public Proof-of-Antiquity rules distinguish generic ARM devices from Apple Silicon. This short explains the project's stated anti-flood rationale and the published 0.0005× versus 1.20× base multipliers without turning those policy choices into unsupported performance or earnings claims.
