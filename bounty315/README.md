## 10 ready-to-post hooks

1. **Closet computer** — That old laptop in your closet is not automatically obsolete. RustChain gives real hardware a reason to stay useful: prove what the machine actually is, then let its age count. **CTA:** Dust off one machine and start with `pip install clawrtc`.
2. **Upgrade rebellion** — The upgrade cycle says the newest computer wins. Proof of Antiquity flips that instinct: a PowerPC G4 carries a 2.5× reward multiplier while modern hardware is the 1.0× baseline. **CTA:** Before replacing old hardware, see what your existing machine can prove.
3. **Second career** — Your 2009 laptop may be retired from daily work, but retirement does not have to mean landfill. A RustChain dry-run can tell you how its hardware fingerprint looks without committing to mining. **CTA:** Run the dry test first.
4. **No GPU farm** — You do not need a warehouse full of GPUs to understand RustChain. Start with one physical computer, one hardware fingerprint, and one honest compatibility check. **CTA:** Use the machine you already own.
5. **Old is the feature** — Most technology products apologize for old hardware. RustChain makes hardware age part of the protocol design. **CTA:** Find the oldest working computer in your house and check the miner docs.
6. **Keep the charger** — Before you recycle that old Mac or PC, find the charger. Proof of Antiquity is built around keeping verifiable physical machines useful instead of pretending every participant is identical. **CTA:** Give one machine one more job.
7. **Dry-run before mining** — Curious but not ready to mine? Good. Test first. The miner's dry-run shows hardware details and fingerprint results so you can see what the software detects. **CTA:** Start with evidence, not hype.
8. **Vintage twist** — A G4 at 2.5×. A G5 at 2.0×. Modern hardware at 1.0×. That reward table is the sentence that makes RustChain different from “new hardware wins” mining. **CTA:** Compare your own hardware before buying anything.
9. **Reuse is practical** — E-waste reduction is easier to care about when reuse has a concrete job attached to it. RustChain's pitch is simple: keep real machines running, prove their identity, and make age economically relevant. **CTA:** Reuse before replace.
10. **One-command doorway** — The first step is deliberately boring: `pip install clawrtc`. No glossy hardware purchase, no special mining rig—just a machine you already own and a compatibility check. **CTA:** Try the dry-run on one spare computer.
